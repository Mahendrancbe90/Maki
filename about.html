About Mahendran

Lead UI/UX Designer, 13+ years in the details that make products work.

I'm a UI/UX Designer based in Tamil Nadu, India, currently Lead UI/UX Designer at AES Technologies in Coimbatore — designing web, mobile, and enterprise interfaces for going on 14 years now.

My process runs on user research, wireframing, and prototyping — turning business requirements into interfaces that hold up under real use, not just in a design review. Across 500+ websites and mobile apps and 50+ software products, the goal has stayed the same: take something complicated and make it feel obvious.

Working Principles

Three things I don't compromise on.

01

Structure Before Style

Information architecture and user flows come before any visual decision — a beautiful screen built on a broken flow is still broken.

02

Systems, Not Screens

I design components and patterns that scale, not one-off screens that break the moment a new use case appears.

03

Design With Constraints

Engineering, timeline, and business constraints aren't obstacles to design around — they're part of the brief.
