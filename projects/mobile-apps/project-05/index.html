Tennis Coach AI

Tennis Coach AI is a sports training mobile app built around AI/ML-powered motion tracking, with progress dashboards, performance analytics, and gamified user flows designed to keep trainees engaged.

Role
Lead UI/UX Designer
Services
Mobile App Design, UI/UX Design
Platform

Problem

The Challenge

Motion-tracking sports data is only useful if a trainee can actually understand and act on it — raw performance analytics needed to become something motivating, not just a wall of numbers.

Goal: Turn AI-generated motion tracking and performance data into a training experience trainees actually want to keep opening.

Process

Design Process

Designed progress dashboards and performance analytics around clear, digestible visualizations, then layered in gamified flows to reinforce consistent training habits rather than one-off sessions.

Solution

UI / UX Details

An energetic, sport-focused visual style with live on-court overlays (shot analysis, player tracking, rally stats) balanced against approachable, game-like progress indicators.

Outcome

Increased active usage among trainees by 45%.
